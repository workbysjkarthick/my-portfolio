About

Modernizing the web experiences people use to manage their money.

I'm a Senior Developer and Assistant Consultant at Tata Consultancy Services, where I've worked since February 2014. My work centers on the BFSI domain: credit card portals, payment flows, chatbots and marketing sites for banks like Citigroup and Wintrust Financial.

I enjoy taking aging, third-party-heavy applications and turning them into maintainable, secure systems built on modern frameworks such as Angular and Vue.js. Along the way I design solution architecture, build components and APIs, and make sure every release works for keyboard and screen-reader users.

Beyond code, I run code reviews and lead training for trainees and new team members, work closely with clients through the Agile Scrum process, and care about delivering exactly what the client expects.
